Спроба 1 · Seaborne Town Centre, 1995 → 2025

The two maps below show the coastal town of Seaborne in 1995 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

ІнструкціяТри кроки: introduction → overview → body. Спочатку читай англійську підказку, потім пиши. Після трьох частин відкриється повна модель.

Крок 1 · Introduction

HintThree moves: show → illustrate; name the place + years; add the time span ("over the 30-year period").

Sample: The two maps illustrate how the coastal town of Seaborne changed between 1995 and 2025.

Крок 2 · Overview

HintOverall impression (fishing village → tourist resort?) + most affected zones (shoreline, former woodland) + what stayed (the sandy beach). No feature names.

Sample: Overall, Seaborne underwent extensive redevelopment over the 30-year period, transforming from a quiet fishing village into a tourist resort. The most dramatic changes occurred along the shoreline and on the land formerly covered by woodland, while the sandy beach itself remained untouched.

Крок 3 · Body 1 + Body 2

Hint · groupingBody 1 = the shift to tourism: jetty → yacht marina, fish market → shopping & leisure centre, wasteland → beach promenade. Body 2 = woodland → apartment complexes, surface car parks → underground parking, and the unchanged beach. Anchor every direction; past simple throughout.

Sample Body 1: The most striking changes occurred along the shoreline. The small boat jetty immediately south of the lighthouse was replaced by a large yacht marina, while the fish market gave way to a shopping and leisure centre. In addition, the wasteland to the east of the main road was transformed into a beach promenade lined with palm trees.

Sample Body 2: Further inland, the woodland in the eastern part of the town was cleared to make way for several apartment complexes and a five-star hotel. The two surface car parks were removed, and a single underground car park was constructed in their place. By contrast, the sandy beach and the seafront itself remained completely unchanged throughout the period.

§1 The two maps illustrate how the coastal town of Seaborne changed between 1995 and 2025.

§2 Overall, Seaborne underwent extensive redevelopment over the 30-year period, transforming from a quiet fishing village into a tourist resort. The most dramatic changes occurred along the shoreline and on the land formerly covered by woodland, while the sandy beach itself remained untouched.

§3 The most striking changes occurred along the shoreline. The small boat jetty immediately south of the lighthouse was replaced by a large yacht marina, while the fish market gave way to a shopping and leisure centre. In addition, the wasteland to the east of the main road was transformed into a beach promenade lined with palm trees.

§4 Further inland, the woodland in the eastern part of the town was cleared to make way for several apartment complexes and a five-star hotel. The two surface car parks were removed, and a single underground car park was constructed in their place. By contrast, the sandy beach and the seafront itself remained completely unchanged throughout the period.

Порівняй рішення: чи є якір у кожному напрямку? Чи є фраза «без змін»? Чи різні дієслова змін (replaced/gave way/transformed/cleared/constructed)?

Спроба 2 · Port Elizabeth, 1985 → 2020

The two maps below show the village of Port Elizabeth in 1985 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Єдина підказка · groupingBody 1 = три зміни, що перетворили рибальське село на курорт (fish market → restaurants & shops; cottages → hotel complex; open fields → golf course). Body 2 = coast road → promenade + два незмінні орієнтири (harbour, lighthouse). Заверши аналітичною фразою («now serves a very different purpose»).
ІнструкціяПиши всю відповідь одразу (§1–§4, 150+ слів). Без таймера. Після чек-листа відкриється модель.
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю

§1 The two maps compare the layout of the coastal village of Port Elizabeth in 1985 with its appearance in 2020.

§2 Overall, the village underwent substantial redevelopment along its shoreline, with several original features replaced by tourism-related facilities. Despite this, two landmarks close to the harbour remained exactly as they had been 35 years earlier.

§3 The most striking changes occurred near the harbour, which itself remained in use throughout the period. The fish market immediately to its south gave way to a row of restaurants and shops, while the cottages to the north-east were demolished and replaced by a large hotel complex. Similarly, the open fields further south were developed into a golf course.

§4 In contrast, the lighthouse at the eastern tip of the village was left untouched, preserving the village's historic character. The narrow coast road along the shore was widened considerably, becoming a promenade lined with visitor facilities. Consequently, although the harbour itself is unchanged, the surrounding area now serves a very different purpose.

Порівняй рішення: Body 1 = саме три «туристичні» зміни? «immediately to its south» — якір? Фінал із «consequently»?

Спроба 3 · Elmsworth town centre, 1960 → 2010 · exam conditions

The two maps below show the centre of Elmsworth in 1960 and 2010. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Інструкція · режим іспиту3 хв — аналіз: знайди рідкісний тип зміни (що сталося з дорогою?), три великі обміни, незмінний об'єкт. 15 хв — письмо. 2 хв — перевірка: якір у кожному напрямку? жодного «was became»? одна фраза «без змін»? Після чек-листа — модель і перепис.
20:00
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю (усі 8 — чесно)

§1 The two maps illustrate how the centre of Elmsworth changed between 1960 and 2010.

§2 Overall, the town centre was substantially modernised, with several buildings replaced and the main road closed to traffic. The most significant change was the conversion of the central area into a pedestrianised zone, while the church remained the one constant feature throughout.

§3 The most significant development involved the old cinema, which was demolished and replaced by a shopping mall considerably larger than its predecessor. Next to it, the former bus station gave way to a landscaped pedestrian plaza. Perhaps most notably, the through-road that once ran across the centre was closed to traffic, transforming the area into a pedestrianised zone.

§4 Further changes took place to the south. The row of shops was extended into a larger retail street, and the market square was redeveloped into a public square, beneath which a car park was constructed. Meanwhile, the church in the north-west corner underwent no alteration, standing exactly where it had stood half a century earlier.

Порівняй рішення: «was closed to traffic» — рідкісне дієслово зміни? «considerably larger than its predecessor»? «beneath which» — деталь без нового речення?
