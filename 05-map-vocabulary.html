1 · Одне правило: дві мапи, один рух

Найнатуральніший підхід: past simple для ВСІХ завершених змін на обох мапах. Present perfect — лише стилістична опція. Ніколи не змішуй обидва для однієї зміни.

3 · Чотири типи змін + тиха категорія

ТипДієсловаПриклад
BUILT / CONSTRUCTEDwas built · was constructed · was developed into · was established · was erectedA large housing estate was constructed where a forest once stood.
DEMOLISHED / REMOVEDwas demolished · was knocked down · was cleared · disappeared · was torn downThe old railway station was demolished and replaced by a car park.
REPLACED / REDEVELOPEDwas replaced by · gave way to · was redeveloped into · was transformed into · made way forThe small market gave way to a large shopping centre.
EXPANDED / IMPROVEDwas extended · was expanded · was widened · was upgraded · underwent significant developmentThe main road was widened into a dual carriageway.
«Без змін» (тиха категорія)remained unchanged · was left untouched · underwent no alteration · stayed exactly as it wasThe church remained completely unchanged throughout the period.

Правило: одне дієслово — максимум двічі на текст. У кожній категорії 4–5 варіантів. Одна фраза «без змін» на текст — обов'язкова: це знак контролю, а не ще один «was built».

4 · Overview language

ФразаПриклад
underwent substantial/considerable transformationOverall, the town underwent substantial transformation between 1990 and 2025.
developed significantly over the periodGreenfield developed significantly over the 35-year period, expanding in all directions.
previously undeveloped land gave way toPreviously undeveloped land on the outskirts gave way to residential and industrial zones.
the most striking changes occurred in/toThe most striking changes occurred to the north and east of the town centre.
underwent significant urban developmentThe town underwent significant urban development, particularly on its periphery.
Overview = масштаб + характер + зони. Заборонено: назви об'єктів, точні локації, «was built».

5 · Introduction — 3 варіанти

ВаріантТекст
1 · how... changedThe two maps illustrate how the town of Greenfield changed between 1990 and 2025, showing considerable urban and infrastructural development over the 35-year period.
2 · compare the layoutThe maps compare the layout of Greenfield in 1990 with its appearance in 2025, revealing the extensive development the town underwent over three and a half decades.
3 · depict the transformationThe two maps depict the transformation of Greenfield between 1990 and 2025, documenting the significant changes that occurred to both the town's infrastructure and land use.

Завжди називай часовий проміжок — він рамка для всієї відповіді.

6 · Шаблони body + зв'язки

  • Рамка однієї зміни: [anchored location], [old feature] + was + change verb + [new feature], + participle clause (reflecting the town's growth / making way for further expansion / thereby doubling its size).
  • Opener Body 1: The most significant development took place to the north of the town…
  • Opener Body 2: Several further changes occurred elsewhere. · In contrast,… · Meanwhile,…
  • Зв'язки: Similarly / In addition (додати) · In contrast / Whereas (контраст) · Elsewhere in the town, / Further to the south, (нова зона) · In its place / Consequently (послідовність).
  • Фрази «було»: the original market · the former bus station · on land that was formerly forested · where a forest once stood · previously occupied by cottages.
